# Learn University: student pilot handbook

Source for the Heard Assistant on the "Learn University" student pilot. This is a student-run pilot,
not an official university service, so this file holds no hostel rules, timings or fees: the assistant
must hand those questions to the student's peer mentor. Add a fact here only when it is confirmed.

## About this pilot
- Heard is being tried by a small group of students for a few weeks (November 2026). It is run by
  students, not by the university.
- Once a week you get a check-in: four taps (studies, money, health, home) and an optional note.
- Only your own peer mentor sees your answers. The organiser sees counts only, never your notes.
- You can stop any time, and ask the organiser to delete your data. It will be removed from Heard.

## Who to ask
- Hostel rules, mess, fees, leave, maintenance: ask your hostel office or warden directly. Heard does
  not have this information.
- Anything personal, or anything you'd like to talk about: your peer mentor, in the Chats tab.

## Help, any time (national numbers)
- Tele-MANAS mental health helpline (free, 24x7, many Indian languages): 14416 or 1-800-891-4416.
- Emergency: 112.
- Ragging is banned by law. National anti-ragging helpline: 1800-180-5522.
