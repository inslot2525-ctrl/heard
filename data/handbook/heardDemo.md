# Sahyadri College (demo): hostel handbook

Invented for the Heard demo. Not a real institution.

## Mess
- Breakfast 7:30–9:00 am, lunch 12:30–2:00 pm, snacks 5:00–5:45 pm, dinner 7:30–9:30 pm.
- Sunday breakfast is from 8:00 to 10:00 am.
- If you will miss a meal for 3 or more days (home visit, internship), tell the mess office a day ahead to get a rebate.
- Special diets (medical or religious): give a note to the mess manager, Mr. Kale, in the mess office.

## In-time and leave
- Hostel gates close at 10:00 pm. Late entry needs the warden's permission in advance.
- Night-out or home leave: fill the leave form on the warden's desk by 6:00 pm, at least two days before you go. Weekend leave forms close on Thursday 6:00 pm.
- Parents may call the warden's office to confirm leave: 020-0000-0000 (demo number).

## Fees and money
- Hostel fees are paid each semester at the accounts office (ground floor, admin building), 10:00 am–4:00 pm on weekdays.
- If you cannot pay on time, you can ask for an extension of up to 30 days. Write to the accounts office before the due date; no late fine is charged while an extension is approved.
- Earn & Learn: part-time campus jobs (library, labs, office) with a monthly stipend. Ask at the student welfare office, room 104.
- Need-based fee support: ask your mentor; they can recommend you to the scholarship committee. Applications are confidential.

## Rooms and maintenance
- Report repairs (electrical, plumbing, furniture) in the maintenance register at the hostel office; urgent problems (water leak, no power) can be reported to the night guard.
- Wi-Fi: network "Sahyadri-Hostel". Get your login from the hostel office with your ID card. Wi-Fi problems: IT helpdesk, room 12, 9:00 am–5:00 pm.
- Laundry room: ground floor, open 6:00 am–10:00 pm.

## Health
- Campus health centre: next to the main gate, 9:00 am–1:00 pm and 4:00–8:00 pm. A doctor visits daily; first aid is available at the hostel office at all hours.
- For an emergency at night, tell the night guard or the warden immediately. Emergency number: 112.
- Counsellor: free and confidential, at the student welfare office, room 106, Monday to Saturday 10:00 am–5:00 pm. You can book through your mentor or walk in.

## Rules
- Ragging is banned by law and by college rules. Report it to the warden, your mentor, or the anti-ragging helpline 1800-180-5522. Reports are confidential.
- Visitors are allowed in the common room only, 4:00–7:00 pm.
- Cooking appliances are not allowed in rooms.
