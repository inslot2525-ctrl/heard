# Heard: for students (and a 1-minute intro script)

## Intro script (for the warden or a mentor at dinner / in the WhatsApp group)

> "From this week our hostel is trying an app called Heard. Every Monday you'll get a one-tap check-in:
> how are studies, money, health and home: good, okay, or struggling. It takes ten seconds. Only your mentor
> sees your answers, not other students; the warden only sees how many people answered. If something's been hard
> for a while, your mentor might message you to talk. You can write in Marathi, Hindi or English, or many other
> languages, and it's translated. There's also an assistant for hostel questions like mess timings and leave forms.
> Scan the poster, enter your name, and save the login code it shows you. It's a pilot, so tell us what to change."

## How to join

1. **Scan** the poster in your hostel (or open the link from the WhatsApp group).
2. **Write your name** and pick your language.
3. Read the short privacy note and tick **I agree**.
4. **Save your login code** (screenshot it or write it down). You need it if you change phones.

## What it does

- **Monday check-in:** four taps, one optional note, and "I'd like to talk to my mentor" if you want.
- **Your mentor:** message them any time. If you write in Marathi and they read English, it's translated both ways;
  the original is always shown.
- **Hostel channel and Notices:** hostel updates from the warden.
- **Heard Assistant:** ask hostel questions any time (mess, leave, Wi-Fi, fees office). If it can't answer, it
  passes your question to your mentor.

**If you're not safe right now:** call Tele-MANAS **14416** (free, 24×7, many languages) or **112**.

**Your data:** only your mentor sees your answers. Ask your warden to delete your data at any time.
