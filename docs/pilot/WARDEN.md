# Heard: a one-page guide for wardens

**What it is.** Each Monday every student gets a one-tap check-in on their phone: studies, money, health, home
(good / okay / struggling), plus an optional note. If a pattern shows up, for example "struggling with money two
weeks running", or a student stops replying, or asks to talk, their **mentor** gets a card with the evidence and a
suggested first message. The mentor decides what to do. Students can write in Marathi, Hindi, English or other
languages, and messages are translated for the reader.

**What you see.**
- **Pilot so far:** how many students joined, check-ins answered per week, conversations mentors started, safety
  alerts. **Counts only: you don't see students' answers.**
- **Notices:** only wardens can post here; every student sees it.
- **Hostel channels** and the private **Mentors room**.
- **Urgent alerts.** If a student writes something that suggests they may not be safe, or mentions ragging, you and
  their mentor get a message straight away with their name. The student is shown helpline numbers
  (Tele-MANAS 14416, 112). Please agree with your counsellor who acts on these, especially at night.

**Your weekly job (1 minute):** Monday morning, open Heard → **Send this week's check-in**.

**Joining.** Open the link or scan the warden code you were given (keep it private), write your name, choose your
language, tick the privacy notice, and **save the login code** it shows you (you need it on a new phone).

**Deletion requests.** If a student asks for their data to be removed, tell <team contact>. It is done the same day.

**Pilot dates:** <start> to <end>. Questions: <team contact>.
