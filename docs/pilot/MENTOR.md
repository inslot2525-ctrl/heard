# Heard: a one-page guide for mentors

**Your inbox** shows students who may need a conversation, with the evidence:
- **Struggling two weeks running** (e.g. money), or a **sudden drop** from their usual answers;
- **Gone quiet**: no check-in for two weeks after replying before;
- **Asked to talk**, or **asked the assistant for help** with something personal;
- **URGENT**: a message that suggests they may not be safe, or mentions ragging. These are always on top. Contact
  the student now, and follow the plan agreed with the warden and counsellor. The student has already been shown
  helplines (Tele-MANAS 14416, 112).

**What to do with a card (2 minutes).**
1. Read the reason, the 6-week bars and the student's own words.
2. Edit the suggested opening message so it sounds like you, and send it. It goes to the student as you, in your
   name. Keep it warm and specific; don't name a diagnosis or use labels ("depressed", "at risk"): the app will
   refuse those.
3. When it's dealt with, **Mark resolved**. If it doesn't need action, **Dismiss** with a short reason.

**Chats.** You can message any of your students directly, and talk with other mentors in the private Mentors room.
Messages are translated automatically when you and the student use different languages; **the original is always
shown**. Tap "show original" if a translation reads oddly, and ask the student in simple words if unsure.

**What Heard never does:** score students, diagnose, or contact a student without you.

**Joining.** Open the link or scan the mentor code (keep it private), write your name, choose your language, tick
the privacy notice, and **save the login code**. Join before your students so they get assigned to you.

**Time:** about 5 minutes a day. Questions: <team contact>.
